# We are Holm Digital AB 👋

We are a professional team dedicated to web accessibility and regulatory compliance.

## 👥 Who we are

- **[Karin Holm](https://github.com/KarinHolmDigital)**: CEO, Product Owner of a11y-hd. Karin founded Holm Digital to make the digital world accessible to everyone. As product owner, she continues to develop all three a11y-hd packages: engine, standards and components.
- **[Daniel Gustafsson](https://github.com/Labontese)**: CTO, Lead Developer. Daniel created and built a11y-hd, and is responsible for our infrastructure, security and deployment.

We develop **[a11y-hd](https://github.com/holmdigital/a11y-hd)**, a regulatory-compliant accessibility ecosystem. It maps **WCAG 2.1/2.2** failures to **EN 301 549**, the EAA and national accessibility law across 17 jurisdictions, bridging the gap between technical validation and legal compliance.

## 🚀 Projects & Work
- 🛠️ **[a11y-hd](https://github.com/holmdigital/a11y-hd)**: A monorepo for regulatory accessibility testing.
  - [`@holmdigital/engine`](https://www.npmjs.com/package/@holmdigital/engine): Test engine for WCAG and EN 301 549, with CLI and CI reporting.
  - [`@holmdigital/standards`](https://www.npmjs.com/package/@holmdigital/standards): Machine-readable regulatory database with enforcement body lookups.
  - [`@holmdigital/components`](https://www.npmjs.com/package/@holmdigital/components): Accessible React components with a built-in accessibility statement generator in 12 languages.
